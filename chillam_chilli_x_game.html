CHILLI X

A 3D-style mobile horror prototype. Explore a foggy abandoned courtyard, collect three glowing sigils, and find the exit before fear overwhelms you.

  • Left thumb pad: move
  • Drag the right side: look around
  • RUN: move faster (but builds fear)
  • INTERACT: collect nearby sigils or open the exit

Note: This is a first playable prototype with stylized 3D-like graphics rendered directly in your browser. It uses no external libraries.
